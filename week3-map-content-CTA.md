## 1. The one-line claim

> **I build products that get used — not just demos.**

Generated 10 options, picked this one, sharpened it to one sentence. Why it wins:
- One short sentence, not a paragraph.
- States what I prove, not what tools I know.
- Maps straight to my proof: Langganin is live, open source, and you can open it yourself.
- Leaves room for the proof to do the talking one glance below it.

**Supporting subline (pairs with the claim):**
> *"See Langganin, my open source app — it's live right now."*
